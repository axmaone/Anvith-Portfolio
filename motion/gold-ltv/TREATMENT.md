# Gold Loan · Stage 08 — How much can you borrow? (LTV)
Style: **Isometric Infographic** (`iso-infographic`). 60 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · English · no narration (subtitles carry the message, per brief) · music + foley.
Internal induction film for new branch staff. Figures: RBI Lending Against Gold and Silver Collateral Directions 2025 (summaries; see FACTS.md). Gold rate is illustrative and labelled as such.

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **The necklace's journey (chosen).** One tracked object travels a route across one board — scale → rate desk → LTV gate → the counter → the market — and is transformed at each station (weight → value → limit → loan). Ends in a scale reveal: the finished infographic.
2. **Two boards compared.** A ₹2 lakh loan and a ₹7 lakh loan side by side, alternating, to show 85 % vs 75 %. Clear, but halves the screen and loses the step-by-step logic new staff need.
3. **Locked vault filling.** A vault on a fixed frame fills with Isotype coins to each tier. Strong "accumulation" image, but no process — doesn't teach *how* the number is reached.

Chosen: **1**. The induction goal is a procedure, and a route with stations *is* a procedure. Opening: a question written as isometric type on an empty board (not the demo's opening). Ending: the full legend board.

## 2. Logline and arc
A grandmother's necklace becomes a number: weighed, priced, capped, borrowed against — and kept safe even when the gold price falls.
Setup: "How much can you borrow?" → Turn: the value tower is *cut* at the LTV line — you never borrow the whole value → Ending: Priya borrows well inside the limit; a price dip shows why the margin matters; pull back to the whole system.

## 3. Benchmark
- Learn from **Kurzgesagt explainers** (one visual metaphor per step, steady push, scale change when a number gets big) and **Monument Valley** (flat three-tone iso geometry, calm palette, a camera that never breaks projection).
- Don't take: characters, colours, music, any shot.

## 5. Beat sheet / cue map
Tempo 96 bpm (beat 0.625 s, bar 2.5 s), 24 bars = 60 s.
- Bars 1–2 (0–5): solo marimba motif (subject motif: 3 notes up). Necklace lands on bar 2 beat 3 (3.75 s) with a thud.
- Bars 2–5 (5–12.5): shaker + marimba ostinato; one pluck per gram-bar group (quarters → eighths as the grid fills = acceleration).
- Bars 6–8 (12.5–20): bass enters; count-up ticks on eighths; tower lands on bar 8 beat 1 (17.5 s).
- Bars 9–12 (20–30): tier plates on beats; **near-silence** at 25.0 s (one beat) before the cut; paper-tear + whoosh on the slice at 25.6 s; full band returns 26.25 s.
- Bars 13–16 (30–40): lighter texture, glockenspiel motif when Priya's block lifts (35.0 s); music low-passed during focus+context.
- Bars 17–21 (40–52.5): pulse only + low drone during the price dip; **second silence** at 47.5 s before the alert ding.
- Bars 22–24 (52.5–60): full motif, one note per label during pull-out, final chord 58.75 s.
