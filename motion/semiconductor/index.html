STAGE 01 / 06

Purify the silicon.

Silica is refined into ultra-pure, electronic-grade silicon.

STAGE 02 / 06

Grow a crystal. Slice a wafer.

A crystal ingot is grown, sliced thin and polished smooth.

STAGE 03 / 06

Print the circuit with light.

UV light through a mask transfers the pattern.

STAGE 04 / 06

Build it layer by layer.

Deposit adds. Doping changes. Etching removes.

STAGE 05 / 06

Wire it. Inspect it.

Metal pathways connect parts; defects are caught early.

STAGE 06 / 06

Cut, package, test.

Each chip is sealed, then tested before it ships.

Every chip starts as sand.

Six precise stages later, it runs your phone.
