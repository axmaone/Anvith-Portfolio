## Context

At AXMA Learning I led an eight-person team across design, facilitation, research and client operations, working with CXOs and L&D heads on custom interventions.

## How a session is built

Each module follows **ICAD**:

- **Interest**: a physical challenge or an unfamiliar scenario that makes people lean in.
- **Concept**: the behavioural-science model behind what just happened.
- **Application**: teams try it again, now with the model, using canvases and simulations.
- **Debrief**: structured reflection that connects the room to the job.

Activities ranged from build-and-test team challenges to trust and communication exercises, each chosen for the behaviour it surfaces rather than for fun alone.

## Measuring it

Instead of "did you enjoy it?", every programme had a KPI statement and a pre/post measure. For design-thinking work, for example:

- % of problem statements reframed into *How might we* opportunities
- Number of feasible ideas per HMW
- % of ideas moved into prototyping

Each metric came with diagnostic prompts for when it did not move: was psychological safety the blocker? Were decision-makers in the room?
