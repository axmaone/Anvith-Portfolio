## In one line

A lender was hiring for three jobs across five states and had no training system. I designed one: experts write the content once, I shape it into lessons at three levels of depth, and any trainer can deliver it.

## The problem

A large, regionally focused lender needed induction and training for three roles (Customer Service Executive, Relationship Manager and Branch Manager) across **five states**. The content was mostly technical: gold-loan valuation, processing, selling and loan calculation. And it had to work **without depending on any single trainer**.

## The plan, in five steps

Think of it as an assembly line that runs from the experts to the classroom:

1. **SMEs generate and categorise content** into three depths: **L1 → CSE, L2 → RM, L3 → BM**. Same topic, three altitudes. (If SMEs lack time, AI drafts the split and SMEs sign it off, so accountability stays with the experts.)
2. **Structure** each level into one concept per day or half-day, mapped to each role's induction weeks.
3. **Sign-off** on the outline before anything is built.
4. **Build** classroom decks, gamified and experiential activities, case scenarios and role-plays, all through **ICAD**.
5. **Hand off** to trainers with everything standardised, so the programme survives if people leave.

Learners are also split into **Fresh** (start from scratch) and **Existing** (assessed first, then routed), with the placement test as its own designed step.

## ICAD, applied to dry content

Every day moves through **Interest → Concept → Application → Debrief**. For compliance, *Interest* opens with the disaster: the real case where someone skipped a step and it cost the branch. *Application* becomes decision-based: "Does this case pass the rules?"

## Gaps I flagged early

- A feedback loop from learner results back into version 2.
- Regional language localisation across five states.
- Ownership of the master template when a policy changes.

## Measurement

Pre-test → learning → post-test → peer feedback, with KPI statements written as "improvement in [behaviour] by reducing [challenge] in [setting]".
