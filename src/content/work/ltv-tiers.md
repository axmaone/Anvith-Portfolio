## The brief to myself

If the long explainer teaches the idea, what is the smallest thing that *reminds* someone of it the day before an assessment?

## The design

A single bar: 100 % gold value. It splits three times: **85 %, 80 %, 75 %**, each tied to a loan size. Serif numerals for the number you must remember; a hatched remainder for the headroom you must leave.

Editorial typography instead of icons keeps it calm and premium, the same visual language as the long-form explainer, so the two feel like one series.

## Where it lives

Designed to be dropped in as a microlearning recap, a social reel or an LMS nugget between modules.
